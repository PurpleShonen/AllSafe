What the logs should show — and should NOT

  • access.log — both vaults show their POST requests; HTTP logging cannot be opted out of
  • app.log — vault B emits vault_unlock_failed on every wrong PIN
  • app.log — vault A emits nothing on a wrong PIN. That silence is CWE-778
  • Lesson: presence in the HTTP log is not the same as security-event coverage
