How an engagement runs

Onboarded in weeks, not quarters

Most clients reach steady-state monitoring inside six weeks. We do not require a platform migration to start — we begin with the log sources you already have and expand coverage from there.

  • Week 1–2 — Baseline. Asset and identity inventory, log source survey, agreed escalation paths.
  • Week 3–4 — Tuning. Detection content deployed against your estate; noisy rules suppressed with a documented rationale.
  • Week 5–6 — Handover. Live triage with your team shadowing, then full analyst ownership of the queue.
  • Ongoing — Review. Monthly detection coverage report and a quarterly purple-team exercise.

Reporting you can hand upward

Every alert carries its evidence

Analysts close tickets with the query that fired, the raw events behind it, the containment action taken, and the residual risk. Nothing is marked resolved on a hunch.

Clients get a standing read-only view of the queue through the client portal — the same record their auditors will ask for.
