Careers

We hire for curiosity and for the discipline to write down what you found. Certifications help; a good write-up helps more.

Real shift patterns

Four-on, four-off rotations with handover time paid. Night coverage is staffed properly, not covered by an on-call pager attached to a day job.

Time to go deep

One day a fortnight is protected for research, detection engineering, or lab work. It is scheduled, not aspirational.

Training budget that gets spent

An annual allowance for certification or conference attendance, plus a home-lab hardware stipend. Unused budget is a management failure, not a saving.

Our process

Four steps, three weeks, no unpaid take-homes

  • Introductory call. Thirty minutes with a recruiter on the role and what you are looking for.
  • Technical conversation. Ninety minutes walking through scenarios with two practitioners. No whiteboard algorithms.
  • Practical exercise. A timed lab during working hours, run with an engineer present.
  • Team and offer. A conversation with the practice lead, then a decision within five working days.
