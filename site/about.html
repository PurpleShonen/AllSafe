About Allsafe

We started as four analysts who were tired of writing reports nobody read. The company has grown; that complaint still shapes how we work.

Our history

Fifteen years of doing the unglamorous part properly

Allsafe was founded in 2011 by four incident responders who kept being called in after the same avoidable failures — an unmonitored jump host, a service account nobody owned, a backup that had not restored cleanly in two years. The findings were never exotic. What was missing was somebody watching consistently and telling the truth about coverage.

The first office was a rented floor in Wilmington with six desks and a whiteboard of client log sources. We took on managed monitoring because clients kept asking who would watch the alerts we had just spent a month tuning. That practice is now the majority of the business.

Today we run a follow-the-sun SOC across two operations centres, keep an offensive team that reports into the same technical leadership as the defenders, and deliberately stay small enough that the person who wrote your detection content can be pulled onto your incident call.

Mission

To give organisations an honest, continuously maintained picture of what is happening in their environment — and the people to act on it before an incident becomes a disclosure.

We measure ourselves on time to containment and on coverage we can evidence, not on alert volume.

How we operate

Three commitments we hold to

Say what we cannot see

Every monthly report names the log sources we are missing and the attack techniques we would not currently catch. A coverage gap you know about is a decision; one you do not know about is an incident in waiting.

Evidence over assertion

Tickets close with the query, the raw events, and the action taken. If an analyst cannot show why something was benign, it stays open and gets escalated.

One team, both sides

Testers and defenders share leadership, tooling, and retrospectives. Findings from an engagement become detection content within the same quarter.
