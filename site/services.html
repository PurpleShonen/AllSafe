Services

Five practices that share one evidence trail. Engage any of them on their own, or run them together so what the testers find becomes what the SOC watches for.

Managed Detection & Response

A staffed queue, not a dashboard you are left to watch yourself.

Our analysts own triage around the clock. We ingest endpoint, identity, network, email, and cloud control-plane telemetry into your SIEM or ours, write detection content against your actual estate, and escalate with a recommended action already attached. Suppression is always documented — if a rule is muted, you can see who muted it and why.

  • Detection engineering mapped to MITRE ATT&CK, reviewed quarterly for coverage drift
  • Named lead analyst per account, with a standing weekly technical call
  • Containment on your authority, or pre-authorised for agreed scenarios
  • Monthly coverage report showing what we can see and, candidly, what we cannot
  • 24/7 coverage
  • SIEM-agnostic
  • MITRE ATT&CK mapped
  • 6-week onboarding

Penetration Testing

Findings you can act on, with the reproduction steps to prove them.

Web and API assessments, internal and external infrastructure, Active Directory attack-path review, cloud configuration review, and social engineering where the scope calls for it. Every finding ships with impact in business terms, a reproduction path, and a remediation that fits the environment rather than a generic control reference.

  • Rules of engagement agreed in writing before any traffic is generated
  • Live findings channel — criticals are raised the day we find them, not at report time
  • Free retest of remediated findings within 90 days
  • Detection debrief: which of your alerts fired during the test, and which should have
  • Web & API
  • Internal / external
  • Active Directory
  • Cloud config
  • 90-day retest

Incident Response

One number, one hour, one team that has done this before.

Retainer clients get a one-hour callout and a responder on a bridge while the first containment decisions are still reversible. We handle scoping, forensic acquisition, timeline reconstruction, eradication planning, and the written record your regulator, insurer, and board will each want in a different shape.

  • Retainer hours roll into proactive work if you never call — they are not lost
  • Forensic timeline with evidence integrity preserved throughout the chain of custody
  • Tabletop exercises twice a year so the first real call is not the rehearsal
  • Post-incident review that produces detection content, not just a lessons-learned deck
  • 1-hour callout
  • Retained or ad-hoc
  • Forensic acquisition
  • Regulator-ready reporting

Risk & Compliance Advisory

Translating control gaps into a sequence someone can actually fund.

Gap assessments against the frameworks you are held to, audit preparation and evidence packaging, third-party risk review, and security programme roadmaps with realistic effort estimates. We write for two audiences at once: the engineer who has to implement it and the committee that has to approve it.

  • Framework mapping across ISO 27001, SOC 2, NIST CSF, PCI DSS, and sector-specific regimes
  • Evidence libraries maintained continuously rather than assembled the month before audit
  • Risk register that tracks accepted risk with an owner and an expiry date
  • Board-level reporting that survives contact with non-technical readers
  • ISO 27001
  • SOC 2
  • NIST CSF
  • PCI DSS
  • Third-party risk

Security Awareness Training

Built around what is actually being used against your sector this quarter.

Role-targeted training for general staff, developers, finance teams, and executives, plus phishing simulations calibrated to current campaigns rather than a five-year-old template library. Reporting focuses on the reporting rate — how quickly people tell you — because that is the metric that shortens an incident.

  • Short, scenario-led modules; no hour-long compliance videos
  • Simulation programmes tuned so click-rate and report-rate are both tracked
  • Developer track covering secure coding against your own language stack
  • Executive briefings on business email compromise and deepfake-assisted fraud
  • Role-based
  • Phishing simulation
  • Developer track
  • Executive briefings
